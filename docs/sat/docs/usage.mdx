# Usage

Once the SAT app is deployed (see [Installation](./installation/)), everything is
driven from the app itself — there are no notebooks to import or dashboards to
publish.

<Admonition type="note" title="Open the app">
Find it under **Compute → Apps → security-analysis** in your Databricks
workspace, then click **Open**.
</Admonition>

## Step 1 — Complete first-run setup

The first time you open the app it shows a setup screen. Provide:

- **Catalog & schema** — where SAT stores its results. The app creates the schema
  and owns it. Pick a catalog you can access, or type any catalog name.
- **SQL warehouse** — runs the analysis queries and alerts.
- **Account credentials** *(optional, for account-wide data)* — an **Account ID**
  and a service principal's **client ID + secret** (that SP must hold the
  **Account admin** role). These unlock Account/Workspace/Metastore Admin
  detection, account users & groups, and other account-wide reports. They are
  stored as **Unity Catalog secrets** — no secret scope required. Without them,
  SAT reports on the current workspace only.
- **Collection scope & compute** —
  - **Workspaces to scan:** this workspace only, or every workspace the service
    principal can reach.
  - **Job compute:** serverless (recommended) or classic. Classic is offered when
    the workspace supports it; a genuinely serverless-only workspace shows classic
    as unavailable.
  - **Scan notebook & folder permissions:** off by default; turning it on walks
    the whole workspace tree so each collection run takes longer to complete.

<Admonition type="tip" title="Everything is reconfigurable">
You can change any of these later from **Settings** (catalog/schema, warehouse,
model, credentials) and **Data Collection → Manage** (scope, compute,
notebook/folder collection). The collection jobs are re-provisioned automatically
when you change compute or scope.
</Admonition>

## Step 2 — Run data collection

On the **Data Collection** page you'll see SAT's collection jobs (initializer,
permissions data collection, account reports, and the secret scanner). They run
on a schedule, and you can trigger any of them on demand.

> Collection can take several minutes per workspace, depending on account size.

<Admonition type="note" title="The app owns the jobs">
SAT creates and manages these jobs itself under the app's service principal. You
don't import or schedule any notebooks — adjust the schedule, scope, and compute
from the app.
</Admonition>

## Step 3 — View results

All results are in the app:

- **Principal Analysis / Resource Analysis** — who can access what, with
  escalation and impersonation risk. See [Permissions Analysis](./functionality/permissions-analysis).
- **Credential Exposure** — hardcoded secrets in notebooks and cluster configs.
  See [Secret Scanning](./functionality/cluster-secrets-scanning).

Because the app queries **as the signed-in user**, Unity Catalog enforces each
viewer's own grants — people see only the results they are entitled to.

## Step 4 — Configure secret scanning *(optional)*

On the **Credential Exposure** page, open **Scan Settings & Custom Detection
Rules** to:

- **Scan coverage** — scan all notebooks (recommended, so the view reflects your
  complete current exposure) or only notebooks modified in the last *N* days.
- **Custom detection rules** — add your own patterns (name, keywords, regex) on
  top of SAT's built-in detectors. Rules are listed as editable rows you can
  change or delete.

Changes are saved to the scanner's config and take effect on the **next scan
run** — no redeploy and no editing notebooks. More detail on the
[Secret Scanning](./functionality/cluster-secrets-scanning) page.

## Step 5 — Set up alerts *(optional)*

On the **Alerts** page, create email/notification alerts on secret-scan findings
(for example, "any hardcoded credential detected"). Pick the condition, how often
to check, and who to notify. Alerts are standard Databricks SQL alerts, so they
also appear under **Alerts** in the workspace and keep working if the app is ever
removed.

<Admonition type="warning" title="Admins only">
Creating, editing, and deleting alerts is restricted to **workspace or account
admins**. Other users can view alerts and run the read-only "test condition"
check, but the create/edit/delete controls are hidden for them.
</Admonition>
